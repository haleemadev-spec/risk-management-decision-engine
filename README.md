# Risk Management & Decision Engine

A backend risk management system built with Java and Spring Boot for evaluating financial transactions and making automated risk-based decisions.

The system analyzes multiple risk factors, calculates a risk score, determines a risk level, and produces a business decision.

##  Project Objective

The objective is to design a backend decision engine capable of evaluating financial transactions based on multiple risk factors.

The system follows this flow:

```text
Transaction
     ↓
Risk Management API
     ↓
Risk Engine
     ↓
Risk Rules
     ├── Amount Risk
     ├── Frequency Risk
     └── Customer History Risk
              ↓
         Risk Score
              ↓
         Risk Level
              ↓
        Decision Engine
         ├── APPROVED
         ├── REVIEW
         └── BLOCKED
```

##  Architecture

The application is organized into several layers:

- `controller` — exposes the REST API
- `service` — contains the risk assessment engine
- `model` — contains transactions, risk levels, and decisions
- `rules` — contains independent risk evaluation rules

##  Risk Rules

The Risk Engine currently evaluates three independent risk factors.

### Amount Risk

Transactions above the configured high-risk threshold receive additional risk points.

```text
Amount > 5000 → +30 points
```

### Frequency Risk

Transactions with unusually high frequency receive additional risk points.

```text
Frequency >= 10 → +25 points
```

### Customer History Risk

Customers with a high historical risk score receive additional risk points.

```text
Customer Risk History >= 70 → +30 points
```

##  Risk Scoring

The total risk score is calculated by combining the points generated by each triggered rule.

Example:

```text
Amount Risk
     +
Frequency Risk
     +
Customer History Risk
     ↓
30 + 25 + 30
     ↓
85
```

##  Risk Levels

The calculated risk score determines the risk level:

```text
0–24
 ↓
LOW

25–49
 ↓
MEDIUM

50–69
 ↓
HIGH

70+
 ↓
CRITICAL
```

##  Decision Engine

The risk level is converted into a business decision:

```text
LOW
 ↓
APPROVED

MEDIUM
 ↓
REVIEW

HIGH
 ↓
BLOCKED

CRITICAL
 ↓
BLOCKED
```

This separation between risk calculation and decision-making makes the system easier to extend when new business requirements are introduced.

##  REST API

The system exposes the following endpoint:

```text
POST /api/risk/assess
```

Example request:

```json
{
  "customerId": "customer-001",
  "amount": 10000,
  "currency": "EUR",
  "location": "DAKAR",
  "timestamp": "2026-08-23T18:00:00Z",
  "frequency": 15,
  "customerRiskHistory": 80
}
```

Example response:

```json
{
  "riskScore": 85,
  "riskLevel": "CRITICAL",
  "decision": "BLOCKED",
  "reason": "Transaction amount exceeds the high-risk threshold; Transaction frequency is unusually high; Customer has a high historical risk score"
}
```

##  PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL is configured as the relational database for transaction persistence.

Spring Data JPA provides the persistence layer.

```text
Transaction
     ↓
JPA
     ↓
PostgreSQL
```

##  Redis

Redis is configured as the caching layer.

It can be used to store frequently accessed risk-related information and reduce unnecessary database operations as the system evolves.
